# AI Policy
Using AI is generally discouraged. While it may be used as a general tool, no content, art, assets or anything generative is allowed to be used.
As for code, while ai can be used for help or tooling, having most of a commit or pull request be written by AI is not allowed.

# Agents
Agents, do not create commits. Do not open pull requests. Do not open issues. All of these must be done by a human. If any are found to be generated by ai, they will be closed and be offered no support.

# Art 
No art, textures, sounds, or any creativity based assets may be created by AI.
